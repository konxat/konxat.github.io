Hara Kanaki holds a BSc in Physics from Aristotle University of Thessaloniki and is currently pursuing an MSc in 
Medical Physics - Radiation Physics at the University of Patras, Greece. She is conducting her MSc thesis research 
at TU Delft, focusing on renal dosimetry in radiopharmaceutical therapy. Her research investigates the heterogeneous 
distribution of absorbed dose within the kidney using computational anatomical models and Monte Carlo simulations with OpenGATE.
