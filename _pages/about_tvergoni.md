Theodora Vergoni holds a Bachelor's degree in Physics from Democritus University of Thrace and is currently a Master's student in 
Medical Physics at the University of Patras, conducting her thesis research at the Reactor Institute Delft, TU Delft. Her research 
focuses on microdosimetry using realistic cellular geometries of cancerous and healthy cells. More specifically, her work involves 
the virtual irradiation of cellular models with different radionuclides and the comparison of absorbed dose distributions, with 
particular emphasis on mitochondria. The broader aim of her research is to investigate the relationship between radiation exposure 
and mitochondrial response.
